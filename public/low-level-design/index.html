Concurrency for Low-Level Design

Based on Hello Interview's concurrency section · Java examples

What to do when many threads share your objects: spotting shared mutable state, race conditions, locks, atomics and deadlock; handing off work with blocking queues, executors and futures; and sharing scarce resources with semaphores, pools, bounded executors and rate limiters. Labs simulate lost updates, double bookings, deadlock cycles, producer-consumer buffers, connection pools and token buckets.
